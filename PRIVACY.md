# Omalook Privacy Policy

_Last updated: October 2, 2026_

Omalook is a desktop email, calendar and contacts client that runs entirely on your own computer. There is no Omalook server, and the developer never receives your data.

## What Omalook accesses

When you sign in, you grant Omalook permission to use your Microsoft 365 or Google account to:

- read, send, organize and delete your email,
- read and manage your calendar events,
- read and manage your contacts.

This access is used only to show you your own mail, calendar and contacts and to carry out the actions you take in the app.

## How your data is handled

- **Direct connection.** Omalook talks directly from your computer to Microsoft's and Google's APIs. Your mail, events and contacts are not sent anywhere else.
- **No collection.** Omalook has no analytics, telemetry, advertising or tracking, and it does not collect or sell personal data.
- **Local storage only.** Omalook stores your sign-in tokens in your system keyring (or in a private file readable only by you if no keyring is available), the list of accounts you added, and the OAuth client credentials you entered. It does not keep a copy of your mail. Attachments are saved to your Downloads folder only when you ask.
- **No sharing.** Your data is not shared with, sold to, or used by any third party.

## Google API Services User Data Policy

Omalook's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the [Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy), including the Limited Use requirements. Data from Google APIs is used only to provide the features you see in the app, is not transferred to others, and is not used for advertising or to train AI models.

## Your choices

- Remove an account in **Settings → Accounts** to delete its stored tokens from your computer.
- Revoke Omalook's access at any time from your Google account at <https://myaccount.google.com/permissions> or your Microsoft account at <https://myaccount.microsoft.com> (or your organization's Entra admin).
- Delete `~/.config/omalook` to remove all local settings.
